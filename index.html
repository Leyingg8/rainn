handshake

Do I have to coordinate between a roofer and a water restoration contractor?

expand_more

No. Unlike standard roofing companies that only touch exterior shingles, we are dual-licensed and IICRC-certified. We seal the exterior leak and deploy interior structural drying equipment on the exact same visit—giving you one direct team and single-invoice accountability.

pin_drop

How does your mobile service and estimate work across San Diego County?

expand_more

We operate fully equipped mobile dispatch units serving North County, East County, Coastal, and South Bay. Our certified estimator arrives on-site with drone diagnostics and moisture mapping tools, providing you a complimentary photo inspection and itemized quote with zero obligation.

receipt_long

Does insurance cover both the roofing repair and interior moisture dry-out?

expand_more

Yes. In storm and sudden water intrusion claims, policies typically cover exterior restoration alongside interior mitigation. We provide adjuster-ready Xactimate photo documentation and bill carriers directly to streamline approval and minimize out-of-pocket stress.
